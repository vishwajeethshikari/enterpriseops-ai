# EnterpriseOps Support Policy

Version: 1.0  
Status: Sample policy for development

## Delayed Orders
- Verify the latest order status before contacting the customer.
- Check available shipment and tracking information.
- Do not invent a reason for a delay.
- Do not promise a delivery date unless it is confirmed.
- Escalate cases when the available information is insufficient.

## Customer Communication
- Use polite and clear language.
- Explain confirmed facts only.
- Do not expose internal system information.
- Record the recommended next action.

## Support Ticket Priorities
- LOW: General questions with no urgent impact.
- MEDIUM: An unresolved order issue requiring investigation.
- HIGH: A serious customer-impacting issue requiring urgent review.
- Choose priority based on the available evidence, not assumptions.
